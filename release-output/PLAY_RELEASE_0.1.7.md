## What parents should know

- Choose **India** in grown-up settings, then open Explore for Hindi Learning and Discover India.
- Hindi and English place names use the device’s installed offline speech. That is synthesized speech, not reviewed human recordings. A missing voice is shown on screen.
- Finished levels stay replayable. New levels after the five free completions in each game still need Premium.
- Changing country hides regional modules and does not delete history.
