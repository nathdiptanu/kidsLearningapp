SproutLab Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026 · Applies to the SproutLab Android app and this page

Questions or requests: ask.artha.support@gmail.com

In short: SproutLab is a learning app for children aged about 3 to 7, set up by a parent or guardian. It does not need an account. Learning progress stays on the device. The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no chat, and no developer server. We do not collect, sell, or share children's personal information.

1. Children and parents

A child does not give an email address, phone number, date of birth, school, address, photo, or voice recording. A parent picks a broad learning band instead of a birth date. The optional nickname stays on the device; please use a first name or alias rather than a full name.

The app has no public profiles, messaging, or child-to-child contact. Settings, learning notes, and Premium sit in a grown-up area that opens with a three-second press and hold. This only checks that an adult means to open it; it does not identify anyone.

None of this is sent to us. The app does not use the advertising ID, location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or SMS.

A parent picks the learning region; the app does not work it out from location, the network, or the SIM. The region only decides which activities appear. It is not a statement about nationality, religion, home language, or ability, and changing it does not delete progress.

Activities are chosen by fixed rules inside the app. SproutLab does not use a remote AI service. Parent summaries describe practice in the app; they are not a medical, intelligence, or school assessment, an IQ score, or a comparison with other children. Optional downloadable packs are not part of this version.

3. Permissions

The Android package still declares internet access, network status, and Google Play Billing because the official billing plugin is present. This build has no store product identifier, so the app does not start a purchase, restore, or subscription-settings request. Learning works without a connection. Sounds and music are built into the app and do not use the microphone. This version does not send notifications.

4. Purchases and Google Play

Five completed new levels in each existing game type are free. Finished levels can be played again. Hindi and Discover India samples, when a grown-up has turned them on, do not use those five free counts and do not have a separate purchase. Premium is planned as an optional annual subscription through Google Play. A store product is not connected in this build, so no parent can buy, restore, or manage a subscription in the app yet. When a product is connected, Google will process payment under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms. We never receive card details. The app would then receive purchase status on the device only. It does not send that status to a server of ours.

Google Play may later give us general app statistics, crash reports, and tester feedback through Play Console. Those reports come from Google, not from code that we added to the app.

5. Email and links

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments. We use them only to answer you. Please do not send a child's full name, school, photos, or learning history. We delete support emails within 12 months after the request is resolved, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

This build does not open an external link from the Premium screen. When a store product is connected, the only link that leaves the app will open Google Play's subscription page from the grown-up area.

6. Deleting data

Progress and settings stay on the device until you remove them. To delete them, open Android Settings → Apps → SproutLab → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for SproutLab, so the app's data is not copied to Google Drive or a new phone. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore lost progress or delete it remotely.

Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel Premium in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

7. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The database is not separately encrypted, so please protect the device with a screen lock and keep it up to date.

8. Your choices and rights

You can change settings, change the learning band, use an alias, or delete data at any time. If you believe a child sent us personal information, or you want to ask about, correct, or delete information we hold from email, write to ask.artha.support@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

9. This page

This page has no analytics, ads, cookies, or external fonts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes visitor information such as IP addresses to run the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this policy and the effective date before that version is released, and ask for any consent the law requires.
